How we work

Step 1.

Understand the actual problem

A conversation, not a questionnaire. Often the stated problem isn't the expensive one.

Step 2.

Scope something shippable

We agree on one thing that will work and what "working" means before anything gets built.

Step 3.

Build it

Short cycles, working software early, no six-month reveal at the end.

Step 4.

Hand it over

You own it, you can run it, and you know what to do when it breaks.

Who we work with

Independent businesses — retail, manufacturing, healthcare, education. Usually somewhere between "the spreadsheets stopped working" and "we can't hire our way out of this." We're small and we take on work we can actually finish.
